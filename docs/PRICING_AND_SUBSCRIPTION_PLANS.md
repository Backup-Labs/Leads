# Pricing and Subscription Plans — LeadFlow Project Concept Note

## 1. Pricing Strategy

LeadFlow will use a tiered monthly Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) subscription model designed to be affordable for small businesses while scaling with growing sales teams and agencies. The pricing structure aligns with a zero-upfront-investment startup strategy by prioritizing self-service onboarding, clear usage limits (user seats and active leads), and low-cost core infrastructure at launch.

At launch, LeadFlow will offer three publicly available subscription plans:

- **Starter** — \$19/month
- **Professional** — \$49/month
- **Agency** — \$99/month

In addition to the three public launch plans, a fourth tier — **Business (\$199/month)** — will be offered as a custom or enterprise package upon consultation for established organizations with larger sales teams and advanced operational needs.

> **Note:** Subscription pricing, feature inclusions, and usage limits may be refined following early customer feedback, pilot testing, and market validation.

## 3. Subscription Packages and Inclusions

### A. Starter — \$19/month
**Target Customers:** Individuals, freelancers, startups, and small businesses that need a simple, structured system to organize customer inquiries and daily follow-ups.

**Included Features and Limits:**
- 1 user account
- Up to 100 active leads
- Centralized lead management
- Basic lead profiles and contact information
- Standard sales pipeline
- Lead status tracking
- Follow-up scheduling and reminders
- Basic quotation tracking
- WhatsApp click-to-chat
- Basic sales dashboard
- Basic lead activity history

**Main Value:** Helps small businesses organize incoming customer inquiries in one place and avoid losing potential customers due to forgotten follow-ups.

---

### B. Professional — \$49/month
**Target Customers:** Growing small and medium-sized businesses with multiple employees involved in sales, quotations, and customer follow-up.

**Included Features and Limits:**
- Everything in **Starter**, plus:
- Up to 5 user accounts
- Up to 1,000 active leads
- Lead assignment to team members
- Customizable sales pipeline stages
- Quotation management and status tracking
- Overdue follow-up alerts
- Lead source tracking (WhatsApp, phone, website, referral, social media, walk-in)
- Team performance overview
- Conversion rate tracking
- Basic revenue pipeline reporting
- Role-based user permissions
- CSV/Excel lead import and export

**Main Value:** Helps growing businesses coordinate a small sales team, track quotations from creation to decision, and improve lead-to-sale conversion rates.

---

### C. Agency — \$99/month
**Target Customers:** Sales-driven businesses, marketing agencies, real estate firms, service companies, and organizations managing high lead volumes across multiple sales representatives.

**Included Features and Limits:**
- Everything in **Professional**, plus:
- Up to 15 user accounts
- Up to 5,000 active leads
- Advanced revenue recovery alerts for cold leads and unanswered quotations
- Advanced sales and conversion analytics
- Sales representative performance comparison
- Lead response time tracking
- Quotation aging and follow-up tracking
- Custom tags, filters, and lead segmentation
- Multi-pipeline support or department pipelines
- Priority customer support
- Automated follow-up workflow rules

**Main Value:** Helps active sales teams prevent revenue leakage, monitor representative accountability, and recover stalled deals before they are lost.

---

### D. Business (Custom / Enterprise) — \$199/month
**Availability:** Available upon consultation as a custom or enterprise offering (not presented as one of the three standard self-service launch plans).

**Target Customers:** Established companies, larger sales departments, and multi-branch organizations that require higher capacity, custom workflows, and dedicated support.

**Included Features and Limits (Subject to Customer Agreement):**
- Everything in **Agency**, plus:
- Up to 40 user accounts
- Up to 20,000 active leads
- Multi-branch or department management
- Advanced role and permission controls
- Custom reporting and executive dashboards
- API/webhook integration support
- Dedicated onboarding and staff training
- Custom workflow setup
- Priority technical support

**Main Value:** Provides established organizations with a scalable, tailored lead and quotation management environment across multiple teams or branches.

## 4. Customer Upgrade Path

LeadFlow's tiered structure is designed so that customers naturally upgrade as their lead volume, team size, and reporting needs grow:

1. **Starter to Professional (\$19 to \$49/month):**
   A business begins on the **Starter** plan with a single founder or salesperson managing inquiries. As the business hires additional staff or exceeds 100 active leads, it upgrades to **Professional** to assign leads across up to 5 team members, customize pipeline stages, track quotation statuses, and monitor team conversion rates.
2. **Professional to Agency (\$49 to \$99/month):**
   As a company scales its marketing and sales operations beyond 5 users or 1,000 active leads, managing follow-up speed and unanswered quotations becomes critical. Upgrading to **Agency** unlocks capacity for up to 15 users and 5,000 active leads, along with automated revenue recovery alerts, representative performance comparisons, response time tracking, and multi-pipeline management.
3. **Agency to Business (\$99 to \$199/month — Custom / Enterprise):**
   Established organizations expanding across multiple branches or managing up to 40 staff members and 20,000 active leads transition to the **Business** package through direct consultation. This provides multi-branch governance, custom executive reporting, API/webhook integrations, and dedicated staff onboarding.

## 5. Additional Revenue and Add-On Opportunities

Beyond base monthly subscriptions, LeadFlow can generate incremental revenue through optional add-ons and billing options as the platform matures:

- **Additional User Seats:** Customers who need extra team members without moving to the next full subscription tier can purchase additional user seats at a fixed monthly rate per user.
- **Official WhatsApp Business API Automation:** Early versions of LeadFlow include **WhatsApp click-to-chat** at no extra messaging cost, allowing users to open direct WhatsApp conversations from a lead profile. In later phases, official WhatsApp Business API automation (automated templates, shared team inbox, and delivery tracking) can be offered as a paid add-on, as official API usage incurs external provider and per-message charges.
- **Premium Third-Party Integrations:** Optional paid connectors for external accounting software, ERP systems, payment gateways, and marketing platforms.
- **Advanced Analytics and Custom Reporting Add-Ons:** Specialized industry reports, scheduled executive exports, and deeper forecasting modules for data-driven teams.
- **Annual Subscription Packages:** Customers who commit to annual billing (12 months paid upfront) may receive a discounted rate (for example, one to two months free), improving upfront cash flow and customer retention.
